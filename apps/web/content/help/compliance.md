> [!CAUTION]
> This page is a general overview, not legal advice. Laws differ by country and change over time. If you're unsure whether you may email a group of people, ask a lawyer who knows your market.

## What Pawpost does for you

Every email Pawpost sends:

- comes from a real mailbox with an honest sender name and address,
- includes your **postal address**,
- includes a working **unsubscribe link** and **one-click unsubscribe headers**,
- is never sent to anyone on your **suppression list**.

Unsubscribe requests are honoured **immediately** and across all campaigns.

## What's your responsibility

- **Whether you may contact someone at all.** This is the big one, and it depends on where the recipient is and whether they're a business or a private person (see below).
- **Honest content.** Subject lines must not be misleading, and the email must not pretend to be something it isn't.
- **Where your list came from.** You should be able to explain why you have each address.
- **Data protection.** Contact lists are personal data. Keep them secure and delete them when you no longer need them.

## The main laws at a glance

| Region         | Law                   | In short                                                                                                                                                                             |
| -------------- | --------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| United States  | CAN-SPAM              | Cold email is allowed if headers and subjects are honest, the email has a postal address and a working opt-out, and opt-outs are honoured within 10 business days.                   |
| European Union | GDPR + ePrivacy rules | Emailing private individuals generally needs prior consent. Rules for business contacts vary by country; some allow relevant B2B outreach under "legitimate interest", others don't. |
| United Kingdom | UK GDPR + PECR        | Emailing individuals (including sole traders) generally needs consent. Emailing corporate addresses is allowed with an opt-out, if relevant to their role.                           |
| Canada         | CASL                  | Requires express or implied consent before sending; one of the strictest laws.                                                                                                       |
| Australia      | Spam Act              | Requires consent (express or, in limited cases, inferred) plus identification and an opt-out.                                                                                        |

## Good habits

- Email people in their **professional role**, about something **relevant to their job**.
- Keep a note of where each list came from.
- Make it easy to say no; Pawpost already does.
- If someone asks how you got their address, answer honestly.
- When in doubt about a region, leave it out of the list.
